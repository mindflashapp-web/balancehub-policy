About BalanceHub

BalanceHub is a multifunctional wellness and productivity app that helps you maintain balance in key areas of life. The app combines smart tracking, insightful analytics, and an AI-powered assistant to support your daily routines and long-term goals.

Main Features

  • Financial Tracker – manage your daily expenses, budgets, and spending habits.
  • Health Monitoring – log blood pressure, weight, sleep, mood, and energy levels.
  • Focus & Discipline – daily focus goals, productivity sessions, and progress tracking.
  • Analytics Dashboard – see trends, charts, and the Balance Index for your overall well-being.
  • AI Chat Assistant – receive support, motivation, and coaching tailored to your needs.

BalanceHub is designed to be intuitive, lightweight, and supportive — helping you take small daily steps toward a healthier, more balanced life.
