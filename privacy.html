Privacy Policy for SendReady

Last Updated: February 2026

SendReady (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use SendReady – Video Compressor.

1. Information We Collect

No Account Required. SendReady does not require registration and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifiable personal data in order to use the app.

1.1 Video Files

SendReady processes video files that you manually select on your device. All video compression and conversion occurs locally on your device.

We do not upload, store, or access your videos on external servers.

1.2 Technical & Usage Information

To maintain stability and improve performance, the app may collect limited non-personal technical data such as:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version
  • Anonymous crash reports
  • Performance diagnostics

This information does not directly identify you.

1.3 In-App Purchases

SendReady offers optional in-app purchases (subscriptions). Payments are processed securely by Google Play.

We do not receive or store your payment details (such as credit card information). Google Play handles all billing and payment processing.

2. Advertising

SendReady does not display third-party advertisements.

3. Advertising Identifier

The app does not use the advertising identifier for personalized advertising. Any system-level identifiers are handled by Google Play services in accordance with Google’s policies.

4. Data Storage

All compressed videos are saved directly to your device storage. We do not maintain external servers that store user video content.

If you uninstall the app, locally stored app data may be deleted depending on your device settings.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data.

Limited technical data may be processed by trusted third-party services (such as Google Play services or Firebase, if enabled) solely for app functionality, crash reporting, and subscription validation.

6. Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect data processed within the app. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

7. Children’s Privacy

SendReady is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws (such as GDPR or CCPA).

Since video files are stored locally on your device, you may delete them at any time directly from your device.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The “Last Updated” date reflects the latest revision.
