Privacy Policy for BalanceHub Last Updated: November 2025 Welcome to BalanceHub (“we”, “our”, “the App”). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing full transparency regarding how your information is handled. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have regarding your information. 1. Information We Collect 1.1. Personal Data BalanceHub does not require an account, registration, or personal identification. The App does not collect your name, email, phone number, address, or any other personally identifiable information. The only case in which we may receive personal data is if you contact us voluntarily (e.g., by email). 1.2. Health & Wellness Data (Stored Locally Only) BalanceHub allows users to record and track various wellness indicators, including: Mood Sleep quality Energy level Blood pressure Weight Focus levels Intervention logs (timers, sessions) All such data is stored locally on your device (via secure local storage / Hive). We do not transfer, upload, store, view, or sell this data. It never leaves your device. 1.3. Financial Data (Stored Locally Only) You may store budget information and spending logs inside the App. These values are also kept locally on your device only and are never transmitted to any server. 1.4. Usage Data (Non-Personal) To improve stability and performance, the App may collect anonymized technical information such as: device type operating system version anonymized crash data basic analytics (if enabled) This data does not identify you personally. 1.5. AI Chat Messages (Sent to API) When using BalanceHub Chat, your message text is securely transmitted to our AI processing endpoint: https://cool-band-ec78.pogozhevv.workers.dev Purpose: Generate a response from the AI assistant. We do not store your chat messages. We do not use chat data for training models. We do not share chat data with third parties. Your message is processed temporarily and discarded immediately after generating the response. 2. How We Use Information We use collected information for the following purposes: To provide the core functionality of the App To generate AI-powered responses based on your input To store your wellness and finance data locally on your device To ensure App stability and performance To improve the user experience through anonymous analytics We do not sell, rent, or trade any personal information. 3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services BalanceHub does not share your personal wellness or finance data. The App may use third-party services for: AI message processing app performance analytics optional error tracking These services operate under their own privacy policies and receive only the minimum necessary data. 4. Data Retention Health & finance data — stored on your device until you delete the App or reset data manually. AI Chat messages — stored temporarily in RAM for processing and not retained afterward. Technical logs — retained only for as long as required for diagnostic purposes. 5. Security We apply reasonable and modern technical safeguards to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure, and you use the App at your own discretion. 6. Your Rights Depending on your location, you may have the following rights under GDPR/CCPA: Right to access your data Right to delete your data Right to opt out of analytics Right to request clarification regarding stored information Since the App stores most data locally, you can remove all data by uninstalling the App. 7. Children’s Privacy BalanceHub is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in jurisdictions with stricter requirements). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. 8. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. All updates will appear on this page with a revised date. 9. Contact Us If you have any questions, requests, or concerns, please contact us: 📧 mindflashapp@gmail.com
